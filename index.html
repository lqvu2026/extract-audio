Extract Audio

Pull the sound out of any video. Runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Choose a video Tap to browse, or drop a file here (MP4, MOV, M4V, WebM…)
Trim
Play or scrub the video to a moment (the white line on the waveform), then tap “Set start here” or “Set end here”.
Preview plays the video part only (no fades)
Export
Your audio is ready
